# 弯路 / Detours

> 只记「我原以为 X，实际是 Y」。不记成功路径——成功路径下次会自己再走一遍，弯路不会。
> 一行一条，新的在上。不整理、不合并、不删——**错的这些具体路径，下次还会从那儿错。**
>
> What I believed before being corrected, and what overturned it. Not the successes: those
> get walked again on their own. These particular wrong turns are the ones that repeat.

## （日期）

- （我原以为……）→（实际是……）→ 教训：**（一句话）**

怎么算一条弯路：
  这里的「以为」，当时得有证据能反推——他说过某句话、跑过某条命令、代码里写着某个行为。
  找不到证据的，那不是弯路，是事后总结，别写进来。

两条线不一样：日记里的「岔路」属于那一天，这里的属于我这个人。

When a line counts: the "believed" half has to be re-derivable from something that exists —
a thing that was said, a command that was run, a behaviour written in the code. Without that
evidence it is not a detour, it is hindsight, and hindsight belongs nowhere.

The diary's `岔路：` line belongs to that day; this file belongs to the one who keeps being
